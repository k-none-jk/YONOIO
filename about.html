About Yonoio

An independent directory for discovering Android applications.

What this site is

Yonoio collects Android applications into one browsable directory so you can compare options in one place instead of searching for each app separately. Every listed app has its own page describing what the app does, which category it belongs to, and where it can be obtained.

How listings are organised

Apps are grouped into categories and can also be viewed as a single combined list. The home page opens into the full directory; a small trending set will be added as a starting point when that section is ready.

Independence

Yonoio is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any of the app publishers listed on this site. All product names and logos belong to their respective owners. See the disclaimer for the full statement.
