About Yonoio

A directory for gaming apps and Android applications.

What this site is

Yonoio is a place to find gaming apps and related Android listings in one directory. Each listing has its own page with a description, category, and a path to more detail or a download. We aim for pages that load quickly, copy that is written in our own words, and download links that are easy to reach.

Who runs it

The site is managed and curated by Naredra, a webmaster and technical SEO specialist. Yonoio was put together with the reader in mind: slow pages and broken links are frustrating, so listings are organised and kept current as new information comes in. We do not claim that every file is independently certified as safe; you should still review each app before you install it.

What we are working toward

The goal is a reliable directory where you can discover current listings, read what each app offers, and follow a download without extra steps. We watch how the site performs and keep the layout straightforward so browsing stays usable.

Independence

Yonoio is not the owner, developer, or operator of the apps listed here. Product names and logos belong to their respective owners. See the disclaimer for the full statement.

Get in touch

We hope you find the directory useful. If you have questions, technical issues, or suggestions, please reach out through the contact page.
