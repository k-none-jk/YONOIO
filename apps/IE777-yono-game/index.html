About IE777

IE777 is a mobile entertainment app organised as a lobby rather than a single long campaign. From the home screen you can move between Hot, Slots and Casino groups, open a short game tile, and keep a wallet and VIP status in view at the top of the screen.

The Yonoio homepage card for this title currently says “Unlock your ₹200-500 reward bonus.” The welcome overlay in the screenshots uses that same band for new and VIP-tier accounts.
